# Project Scope

## Business problem

Validate whether simulated system billing applies the correct Orion delivery price category, component rate and effective date, then quantify and investigate billing variances.

## Core questions

1. Were billing periods before 1 April 2026 charged using the 2025 schedule?
2. Were billing periods from 1 April 2026 charged using the 2026 schedule?
3. Did each ICP use the price category recorded in its master data?
4. Were fixed and variable delivery charges calculated using the correct units?
5. Which discrepancies caused overbilling or underbilling, and what was the financial impact?

## Included

- Residential Low User: URES and RES
- Residential Standard User: RSU and RSC
- Fixed Daily Charge, Weekend, Peak, Shoulder, Off Peak and zero-rated Super Off Peak components
- February to May 2026 billing periods
- Expected-versus-system billing reconciliation
- Annual price-change validation

## Excluded from the first version

- Retail energy charges and retailer margins
- GST, discounts, payments and debt collection
- Winter Peak Injection and export credits
- Loss-factor calculations
- Streetlighting, general business, irrigation and major-customer pricing
- Any representation of Orion's internal billing system or confidential data

## Data transparency

The 2025 and 2026 price schedules are official public Orion data. All ICP, retailer, consumption and system-billing records will be simulated because individual billing records are not public.
